PlantPulse — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 • Version 1.0
Google Play Data Safety Certified

Welcome to PlantPulse ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data when you use the PlantPulse Android mobile application and connected cloud services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information necessary to provide you with seamless plant health monitoring and telemetry synchronization:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your Name and Email address to authenticate your session and manage your individual plant portfolio. Passwords are securely hashed with industry-standard bcrypt algorithms before storage.
  • Plant & Telemetry Data: Plant species, nicknames, sensor readings (soil moisture levels, environmental temperature, humidity, sunlight lux levels), and watering logs you record.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic device telemetry including network connectivity status to enable offline-first queuing and automatic cloud synchronization when online.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To deliver real-time soil moisture and environmental analytics on your dashboard.
  • To provide automated health status alerts (Optimal, Low Moisture, Over-watered).
  • To synchronize your offline sensor readings seamlessly with your private cloud account.
  • To authenticate and protect your account from unauthorized access.

🔒 Zero Data Sale Guarantee: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or plant telemetry data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We take security seriously and implement stringent technical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the PlantPulse Android app and our cloud API are strictly encrypted using TLS/HTTPS protocol. Plaintext HTTP is permanently disabled.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are hashed with cryptographic salt.
  • Offline Storage Security: Data cached offline on your Android device is stored inside private, sandbox-isolated application storage inaccessible to other apps.

4. Third-Party Services

The application relies solely on our dedicated cloud infrastructure for data storage. We do not integrate third-party ad networks, trackers, or commercial telemetry SDKs that profile your activity.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Deletion of Records: You can delete any plant profile, sensor reading, or logged action directly from the application interface at any time.
  • Full Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated telemetry records permanently, you may request account deletion by emailing us at support@plantpulse.app or via the application settings. All data will be purged within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

PlantPulse is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our app or changes in legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
